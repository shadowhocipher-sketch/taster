Editorial · 05

The one real barrier: 1813 vocabulary

Five words that stop modern readers — and their modern swaps:

  • vex → annoy
  • felicity → happiness
  • imprudence → poor judgment
  • countenance → face / expression
  • amiable → kind, pleasant

Master these five and the prose stops feeling like a costume drama.
